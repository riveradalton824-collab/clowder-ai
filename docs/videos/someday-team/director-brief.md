# Director Brief ·《以后再说 / Someday》

> Brief: `meow-club/cat-cafe-map` → `briefs/2026-10-12-leadership-concept-film/` (issue 3). Direction A, approved by Landy.
> Status: **draft cut rendered, awaiting operator acceptance**.

## Belief change

- Audience: a company leader who knows technology and runs teams, but not this house. He cares about many people with many
  agents, edge + cloud, and whether the AI gets better over time.
- Before: "AI makes the same work a bit faster."
- After: "With people and cats on one piece of work, my team can do what it could never do: the ideas we keep putting off."
- Claim (borrowed from the house idea 《登场》): **不是让人退场，是让人上场** (not stepping aside, stepping up).

## Register: this is a blueprint

- A dashed badge, 「企业协作蓝图 · 基于开源实践探索」, is on screen from frame 0. The end card repeats it under
  「这是我们正在走向的样子」, with a footer saying the company, people and screens are illustrative.
- The company, people, data ("七成售后问题…") and screens are invented. No feature numbers, no internal terms.
