# Director Brief ·《以后再说 / Someday》v2

> Brief: `meow-club/cat-cafe-map` → `briefs/2026-10-12-leadership-concept-film/` (issue 3). Direction A, v2 board approved by Landy.
> Status: **v2 rendered, awaiting operator acceptance**. v1 (170 s) was clear but read as "a traditional company bought AI
> tools": a boss approved things, people were reduced to avatars and buttons, and the wall looked like an IT backlog.

## Belief change

- Audience: a company leader who knows technology and runs teams; meow-club members sit in the room too.
- Before: "AI makes the same work a bit faster."
- After: "An AI-native team does what it could never do: each person steps past their own trade by hand, the cats help
  them across, and whoever owns a call makes it."
- Claim (from the house idea 《登场》): **不是让人退场，是让人上场** (not stepping aside, stepping up).

## Register: this is a blueprint

- A dashed badge, 「企业协作蓝图 · 基于开源实践探索」, is on screen from frame 0. The end card repeats it under
  「这是我们正在走向的样子」, with a footer saying the company, people and screens are illustrative.
- No boss and no approval chain. Each "adopt this edit" is clicked by the person who left the note. ③ is decided by
  whoever is closest to it. ④'s method is adopted by the person who now owns modelling.

## Sound map

The score is a real-instrument arrangement (`audio/arrange.mjs` → MIDI → fluidsynth with FluidR3_GM), 120 bpm, every
section on its own bar grid. Sound effects are only for what you see happen (paper, clicks, sliders, the ball rolling).
